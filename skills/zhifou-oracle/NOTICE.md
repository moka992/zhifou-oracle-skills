## Rider-Waite-Smith artwork

The original 1909 deck artwork is by Pamela Colman Smith, published by Rider. These JPEGs were previously bundled by the Zhifou app from Wikimedia Commons' original Roses & Lilies collection, resized for mobile use. The source collection identifies these historical works as public domain. Preserve `assets/RIDER_WAITE_SMITH.txt` for source information. Do not substitute a modern recoloring or a modern commercial deck under the same notice.

Collection: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite_tarot_deck_(Roses_%26_Lilies)
